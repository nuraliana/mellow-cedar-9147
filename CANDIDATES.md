# Candidates

Five one-sentence pitches I composed for this bounty, scored 1-5 on each
criterion: (1) makes the case to a friend who has never coded, in one read;
(2) persuasion — a concrete reason to say yes now, handing over the idea
feels easy and low risk; (3) clarity — one read is enough, plain words, no
jargon; (4) truth — what it says an AI agent can do with a first app idea
on make.fun must hold up for a beginner, no overpromising; (5) warmth and
craft — sounds like a person talking to a friend, a fresh turn of phrase,
not a template or a slogan.

## Winner: candidate 1

Candidate 1 wins because it is the only one that carries every part of the
pitch in a single breath: the reassurance that the hard part is already
done, the concrete action of describing the idea in plain words, the
platform, a working app to try today, the free cost, and the double safety
of no code from the friend plus nothing they cannot change. Candidate 2 is
warmer in tone but vaguer about the action and drops the stronger safety
line. Candidate 3 is close but "tweak" is a slightly less plain word for a
total beginner. Candidate 4 opens well but "a real app" is less concrete
than "a working app" and repeats the no-code point. Candidates 5 and 6 read
fine but feel flatter, and candidate 5's "type it" makes the step sound
like a form to fill in rather than a conversation.
